# Recall scaffold

The appearances list on lindsaykpino.com only starts in Feb 2026, so everything
earlier has to come from memory. Free recall over ten years does not work well;
recall *between fixed points* does. So each year below lists what the CV already
pins down — dated, verifiable anchors — and then asks what sits in the gaps.

Work newest-to-oldest. Answer in any format; rough notes are fine and I will turn
them into LaTeX. The useful fields are **when / what event / where / talk title /
your role** — and any of those can be "don't remember."
